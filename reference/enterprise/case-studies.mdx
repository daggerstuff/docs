## No case studies are published

This page used to list four "success stories" — a university counseling
program, a regional hospital system, a crisis hotline network and a community
mental health center — with figures such as "40% improvement in trainee
readiness" and "60% reduction in training time".

None of those organizations exists as a customer, none of the figures came from
a study, and the page named no partner. It has been withdrawn rather than
reworded, because there is nothing to reword: the content was invented.

No pilot customer, reference, endorsement or outcome result exists at this time.
When one does, it will be published with the institution's written permission,
the study design, and the measured result it is based on.
