# HIPAA Officer Designations

**Withdrawn — no officers are designated.**

This document previously designated a HIPAA Security Officer and a HIPAA Privacy
Officer for Pixelated Empathy, with an effective date, role descriptions,
responsibilities and authority. None of those designations was made. No person
holds either role for this product, and no HIPAA compliance program is operating.

The training product is scoped to synthetic scenario content and
trainee-authored practice material. It does not accept protected health
information, it is not offered as a Business Associate service, and no Business
Associate Agreement is in force.

If the company later operates a HIPAA-covered service, the designations will be
published here with the named individuals and the date each accepted the role.
