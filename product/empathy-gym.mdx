# The Empathy Gym

The Empathy Gym is a proposed training concept for practicing difficult
conversations with synthetic scenarios. It is for educational practice only; it
does not provide clinical care, diagnosis, treatment, crisis monitoring, or
validated competency assessment.

## Pilot status

**No pilot is currently available for enrollment.** The bounded proposal is
under internal readiness review. Its current planning scope is one training
program, 5–10 trainees, 3–5 synthetic scenarios, and 2–4 weeks. These figures
are planning limits, not a customer offer or commitment.

The proposed practice flow is scenario, conversation, transcript-grounded
feedback, retry, and faculty discussion, subject to implementation and release
verification. Specific scenarios, feedback quality, analytics, access controls,
retention, deletion, security terms, and support have not been approved as
pilot capabilities.

## Boundaries

- Use synthetic training content only. Never enter real client information,
  patient records, or protected health information.
- Do not use the tool to provide clinical care, make diagnosis or treatment
  decisions, or monitor emergencies.
- No accreditation mapping, clinical validation, outcome improvement, or
  competency claim is established.
- Scheduling, EHR, prescribing, payer, and other external integrations are not
  included in the proposed pilot.
- Data, retention, deletion, support, availability, and commercial terms must
  be agreed before any enrollment is offered.

This page describes a proposal only. It does not constitute an enrollment offer
or a claim that any unverified capability is available.
